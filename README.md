## Case study Kasiro - An E-Commerce System

**Organisation:** Kasiro — a simulated Nigerian e-commerce platform selling electronics, fashion and home goods online, with delivery across Nigerian states. *(Simulated organisation; all data is fictional.)*

**Nature of the business:** customers browse a catalogue, place orders, pay by card, bank transfer, USSD, wallet credit or pay-on-delivery, and track delivery through courier partners.

**Users of the database**
- Customers — register, browse, order, pay, review, keep wishlists
- Operations staff — manage stock, follow up pending orders, dispatch shipments
- Finance — reconcile payments and refunds
- Management — sales and category performance reports

**Major data stored:** customers and addresses, wallets, product catalogue and categories, inventory, orders and order lines, payments, shipments, reviews.

**Key operations:** register customer · add/update products · search catalogue · place order and reduce stock · take payment · refund · track shipment · sales reporting.
